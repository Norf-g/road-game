# Слой адаптивной иконки Android

Файл собран скриптом `tools/art.py prompts` из `art/manifest.json` — правки вносите туда.
Каждый запрос копируйте целиком. Результат сохраняйте в `art/inbox/<id>.png`.

## app_icon_foreground

Итог: 432×432 → `game/assets/app/icon_foreground_432.png`

```
Foreground layer of an Android adaptive app icon. Minimalist flat vector illustration made of simple geometric shapes, top-down view. Colors: roads #7C7C84, water #9CCBE8, buildings #F0883E and #4D8FD1. No gradients, no shadows, no text, no letters. All shapes stay inside the central 60% of the canvas. Perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF background filling the whole image, with nothing else on it. Square 1:1.

Subject: The same motif as the app icon: a small orange house connected by one bold winding road to a larger orange building, with a small blue river under a bridge.
```
