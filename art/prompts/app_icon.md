# Иконка приложения

Файл собран скриптом `tools/art.py prompts` из `art/manifest.json` — правки вносите туда.
Каждый запрос копируйте целиком. Результат сохраняйте в `art/inbox/<id>.png`.

## app_icon

Итог: 192×192 → `game/assets/app/icon_192.png`, 512×512 → `art/store/icon_512.png`

```
Mobile game app icon. Minimalist flat vector illustration made of simple geometric shapes, top-down view. Soft pastel palette only: ground #F4EFE6, roads #7C7C84, water #9CCBE8, buildings #F0883E and #4D8FD1. No gradients, no shadows, no text, no letters. Bold and readable at very small sizes, few large shapes. The illustration fills the whole square canvas edge to edge: no border, no rounded frame, no margin. Square 1:1.

Subject: A small orange house in the lower left connected by one bold winding road to a larger orange building in the upper right; a narrow blue river crosses the road diagonally under a small bridge.
```
