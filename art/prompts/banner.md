# Баннер для магазина

Файл собран скриптом `tools/art.py prompts` из `art/manifest.json` — правки вносите туда.
Каждый запрос копируйте целиком. Результат сохраняйте в `art/inbox/<id>.png`.

## store_banner

Итог: 1024×500 → `art/store/banner_1024x500.png`

```
Wide promotional banner for a mobile puzzle game about building roads in a growing city. Minimalist flat vector illustration, top-down view, simple geometric shapes. Soft pastel palette: ground #F4EFE6, roads #7C7C84, water #9CCBE8, buildings #F0883E, #4D8FD1, #F2B33D, #4FB286, trees #8CC084. No gradients, no shadows, no text, no letters. Wide landscape format, aspect ratio about 2:1.

Subject: A small pastel city seen from above: houses of different colors connected by roads, a river with a bridge on the right side, a few round trees. Keep the left third of the image almost empty (plain ground color) for the game title.
```
